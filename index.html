Physical AI and Autonomous Systems Research



Jose M. Alvarez

I lead the Physical AI and Autonomous Systems Research Group at NVIDIA, CA, USA. Our research looks at autonomy at the system level. We develop models that don’t just perceive the world, but also predict it, simulate it, and reason about counterfactuals before they act. They then improve continuously through closed-loop data generation and evaluation. This includes world models, end-to-end driving, vision-language reasoning, and efficient deployment on the edge.
